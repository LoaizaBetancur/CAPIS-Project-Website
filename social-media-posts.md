**Post 2 — Why Participate**

Why should you participate in the CAPIS Project?

Because the outcomes WE choose to measure shape how future training interventions are evaluated. If we don't agree on what matters, we can't compare, combine, or build on research findings.

We need YOUR perspective — players, coaches, practitioners, and researchers — to ensure the core outcome set reflects real-world priorities.

**Tweet 2 — Thread (5 tweets)**

🧵 THREAD: Why the CAPIS Project matters

1/ When researchers study plyometric training in football, they measure different outcomes. This makes it impossible to compare or combine results.

2/ The CAPIS Project is developing a Core Outcome Set (COS) — a minimum set of outcomes that ALL future studies should measure.

3/ We're using a 5-phase methodology: Scoping Review → Focus Groups → Winnowing & Binning → e-Delphi Surveys → Consensus Meeting.

4/ We need YOUR input: athletes (Tier 3-5), coaches, practitioners, and researchers.

5/ Register your interest: https://capis-project-website.vercel.app
